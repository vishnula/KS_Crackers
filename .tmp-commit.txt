Make the price list colourful with per-category illustrations

117 rows of text read as a spreadsheet, and there are no product photos yet.
Every category now has its own colour and a drawn cracker icon - a bomb, a
flower pot, a chakkar spiral, a rocket, a sparkler - used as the category
header, the home-page tile and, crucially, as the row thumbnail wherever a
photo is missing. That fills the hole an absent image leaves and gives
customers a shape to recognise while scanning on a phone. When a real photo is
uploaded it simply replaces the illustration.

Icons are inline SVG on a 24x24 grid rather than image files, so they inherit
the category colour and cost no extra request.

The hero gains animated firework bursts, also inline SVG with a CSS-only
animation so nothing blocks the page, and disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.

Product names now wrap instead of truncating. With a 56px thumbnail taking
column width, names like 4 GOLD LAKSHMI were being cut off mid-word on a 375px
screen, which is unreadable for someone deciding what to buy.

Sold-out rows are greyscaled as well as dimmed, so the added colour does not
make them look available.

Also in this change: the site carries noindex unless NEXT_PUBLIC_ALLOW_INDEXING
is true. The workers.dev demo holds the client's real phone numbers and prices,
and an indexed throwaway domain would compete with the real one later.
